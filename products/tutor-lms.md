# Tutor LMS

> Status: DRAFT — written from public information. Needs review by the Tutor LMS product/brand owner.

## What it is
A WordPress LMS plugin by Themeum (an Ollyo company) for creating, selling and managing
online courses: drag-and-drop course builder, quizzes, assignments, certificates,
monetization and multi-instructor marketplaces. Free core plus Pro.

## Who the users are
- **Primary:** Solo course creators, educators, coaches and trainers who want to sell courses from their own WordPress site.
- **Secondary:** Small/mid training businesses and academies; people running multi-instructor course marketplaces; WordPress agencies building e-learning sites for clients.
- **Technical level:** Mostly non-technical to moderately technical. They know WordPress basics, not code.

## What they care about
- Launching and selling courses quickly, without code.
- Student engagement and outcomes (quizzes, certificates, progress).
- Earning money: monetization, marketplace revenue sharing.
- Owning their platform instead of paying a hosted platform.
- Looking professional to their students.

## Key messages / value props
- Build and sell online courses on your own WordPress site.
- Easy, no-code course building.
- Everything you need for teaching: quizzes, assignments, certificates.
- Grow an academy or marketplace.

## Tone & voice
- **Is:** Encouraging, educational, approachable, empowering. Talks about teaching, learners and growth.
- **Is not:** Corporate, cold, developer-jargon heavy, or "enterprise compliance" in feel.

## Asset-type notes
| Asset type | Expectations | CTA needed? |

| Blog banner | Headline-led; topic illustration or UI snippet; clean. Matches the article's topic. | Usually no |
| Feature announcement | Feature name prominent; show the feature (UI) and the benefit to course creators. | Optional ("Try it now") |

| Landing hero | Benefit headline, product UI, primary CTA. | Yes |

## Common mistakes to flag
- Looks like a generic SaaS ad; nothing says "teaching" or "courses".
- Developer-oriented language aimed at educators.
- Too many feature callouts in one banner.
- UI screenshots too small to read at display size.
- "TutorLMS" or "Tutor Lms" spelling.
