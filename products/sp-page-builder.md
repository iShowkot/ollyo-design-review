# SP Page Builder

> Status: DRAFT — written from public information. Needs review by the SP Page Builder product/brand owner.

## What it is
A drag-and-drop page builder for Joomla by JoomShaper (an Ollyo company). It works as a
component plus module and runs on Helix Ultimate or other templates. It comes with addons,
ready layouts and frontend editing, and integrates with EasyStore for store pages.

## Who the users are
- **Primary:** Joomla site builders: freelancers, agencies and site owners who want to build pages visually.
- **Secondary:** Joomla developers who want faster delivery; long-time Joomla users.
- **Technical level:** Mixed. Comfortable with Joomla admin, not necessarily coders.

## What they care about
- Building beautiful Joomla pages fast without code.
- Lots of addons and ready-made layouts.
- Reliability and compatibility with their Joomla version.
- Responsive results.

## Key messages / value props
- The drag-and-drop page builder for Joomla.
- Build any page visually: addons, layouts, frontend editing.
- Works with your template; pairs with Helix Ultimate and EasyStore.

## Tone & voice
- **Is:** Practical, confident, friendly, productive. "Build faster."
- **Is not:** Overly technical, or WordPress-flavored (this is a Joomla audience).
