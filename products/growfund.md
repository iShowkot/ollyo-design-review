# Growfund

> Status: DRAFT — written from public information. Needs review by the Growfund product/brand owner.

## What it is
A WordPress donation and crowdfunding plugin by Themeum (an Ollyo company), the renamed
successor of WP Crowdfunding. It runs donation campaigns or reward-based crowdfunding
projects from your own site, with no extra platform fees. It has fundraiser/collaborator
roles and a modern React-based dashboard. Same team as Tutor LMS.

## Who the users are
- **Primary:** Nonprofits, charities, NGOs and community organizations collecting donations.
- **Secondary:** Creators, startups and project owners running reward-based crowdfunding; agencies building fundraising sites.
- **Technical level:** Mostly non-technical: fundraisers and organizers, not developers.

## What they care about
- Raising more money for their cause or project.
- Keeping the money: no platform fees, owning donor data.
- Trust and credibility with donors.
- Easy campaign setup and management.

## Key messages / value props
- Raise funds directly from your own WordPress site.
- No platform fees; your data stays yours.
- Donations and reward-based crowdfunding in one plugin.

## Tone & voice
- **Is:** Hopeful, trustworthy, human, impact-focused. Warm but credible.
- **Is not:** Salesy, flashy-tech, cold-corporate, or exploitative of hardship imagery.
