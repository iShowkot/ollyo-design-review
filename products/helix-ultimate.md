# Helix Ultimate

> Status: DRAFT — written from public information. Needs review by the Helix Ultimate / Joomla templates owner.

## What it is
A free Joomla template framework by JoomShaper (an Ollyo company). It is the base for
JoomShaper's Joomla templates and handles global site structure, layout, header/footer,
typography and styling. It pairs with SP Page Builder for page content.

This file also covers **Joomla templates built on Helix Ultimate.**

## Who the users are
- **Primary:** Joomla site builders and agencies who want a solid, customizable template base.
- **Secondary:** Joomla developers building custom templates; site owners choosing a ready-made JoomShaper template.
- **Technical level:** Moderate to high. Comfortable with Joomla.

## What they care about
- Flexible layout and styling control without hacking code.
- Performance, SEO and modern standards.
- Compatibility with current Joomla versions.
- For templates: a beautiful, ready-to-launch niche design.

## Key messages / value props
- The flexible, free Joomla template framework.
- Full control over layout, header, typography and styles.
- Templates: launch a professional Joomla site fast.

## Tone & voice
- **Is:** Reliable, capable, professional, clear.
- **Is not:** Flashy without substance; WordPress-flavored.
