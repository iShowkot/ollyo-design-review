# EasyStore

> Status: DRAFT — written from public information. Needs review by the EasyStore product/brand owner.

## What it is
A modern eCommerce extension for Joomla by JoomShaper (an Ollyo company). It covers
products with variations, inventory, categories and tags, cart and checkout (including
guest checkout), customers, coupons and sales, payments and shipping carriers, reports
and multilingual stores. It integrates with SP Page Builder for storefront and product page design.

## Who the users are
- **Primary:** Joomla site owners and small businesses who want to sell online on Joomla.
- **Secondary:** Agencies and freelancers building Joomla stores for clients.
- **Technical level:** Mostly non-technical to moderate. Shopkeepers, not developers.

## What they care about
- Selling online easily without a complex setup.
- Getting paid and shipping orders (gateways, carriers, invoices).
- A store that looks good and converts.
- Tracking sales.

## Key messages / value props
- Easy, modern eCommerce for Joomla.
- Everything to sell: products, checkout, payments, shipping, coupons.
- Design your store visually with SP Page Builder.

## Tone & voice
- **Is:** Simple, friendly, business-focused, reassuring.
- **Is not:** Technical, complicated, or enterprise-heavy.
