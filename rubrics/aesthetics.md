## A1 — Visual hierarchy (2.5)
Is there a clear 1st → 2nd → 3rd reading order?

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 2.5 | Eye lands on the headline/hero, then supporting element, then CTA/logo — effortlessly. |
| 1.5–2.0 | Order is mostly clear; two elements fight for attention. |
| 0.5–1.0 | Flat — everything has similar weight; or the wrong thing dominates. |
| 0 | No discernible hierarchy. |

Check: size contrast (headline should be clearly dominant), weight, color/contrast
emphasis, position, squint test.

## A3 — Typography (2.0)
| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 2.0 | ≤2 typefaces, clear scale, good line length/leading, readable at display size, on-brand fonts. |
| 1.5 | Good overall; minor issues (tight leading, slightly weak size contrast). |
| 1.0 | Too many styles/weights, awkward line breaks, or borderline readability. |
| 0–0.5 | Hard to read or clashing type. |

Check: awkward rag/widows in headlines, ALL CAPS overuse, text too small for the channel.

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 2.0 | Harmonious, on-brand palette; strong contrast where it matters; color guides attention. |
| 1.5 | Pleasant; slightly off-brand accents or one weak contrast area. |
